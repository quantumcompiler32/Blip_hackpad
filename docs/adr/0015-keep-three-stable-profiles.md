# Keep three stable profiles

Blip revision one has exactly three stable Profile identities—`main`, `coding`, and `research`—cycled by SW11 in that order. Display names, icons, colors, key labels, actions, and Context Browser choices are configurable, but Profiles cannot be added, deleted, duplicated, reordered, or replaced during import. SW11 profile switching and SW12 Control Center behavior remain locked across all Profiles; custom Profiles are deferred until demonstrated need exists.
